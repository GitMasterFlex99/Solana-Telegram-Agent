# UX rules

The product should feel calm and obvious, not like a trading terminal.

## Navigation

Only four primary actions:

- Scan
- Portfolio
- Watchlist
- Settings

## Token card

Show only what helps a decision:

- symbol/name
- simple score
- liquidity
- 24h volume
- 24h change
- age
- risk label
- one short reason

Put deeper information behind Analyze.

## Trading

Never show a giant order form. Offer a few small amount buttons such as `$1`, `$2`, `$3`, then show one confirmation screen with exact spend, expected output, slippage, fees and destination.

## Wallet warning

Before the first connection, show the separate-wallet warning and require acknowledgement. Scanning remains available without a wallet.

## AI

AI is one optional button on Analyze. Do not put AI behind a subscription or make it necessary for basic use.

## Visual direction

Use generous spacing, restrained typography, short copy, clear hierarchy, subtle borders and minimal controls. Avoid dashboards full of widgets, gradients, badges and noisy colors.
